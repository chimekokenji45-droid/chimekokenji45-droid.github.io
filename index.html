USDT GUIDE

What is USDT?

USDT is a cryptocurrency commonly known as a stablecoin. It is designed to maintain a value close to the United States dollar, although cryptocurrency services and markets can change.

USDT can exist on different blockchain networks. Always check which network is supported before sending or receiving funds.

Important: Always verify the blockchain network and destination before sending cryptocurrency.
FAUCETPAY GUIDE

Using FaucetPay

FaucetPay is a cryptocurrency micropayment service used by various faucet websites.

SAFETY

Cryptocurrency safety

  • Never share your wallet private key.
  • Never share your recovery phrase.
  • Never send money to someone claiming they can unlock your faucet balance.
  • Verify cryptocurrency network information before making a transaction.
  • Be careful with websites requesting passwords or recovery phrases.
  • Cryptocurrency transactions may be difficult or impossible to reverse.
