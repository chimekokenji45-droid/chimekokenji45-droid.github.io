How Nexus Faucet Works

1. Create an Account

Register with your email and password to create your Nexus Faucet account.

2. Claim USDT

Claim your faucet reward every 30 minutes.

3. Refer Friends

Share your referral link and earn 20% of eligible faucet rewards from your referrals.

4. Withdraw

Once your balance reaches the minimum withdrawal amount, you can request a USDT payout.

5. Complete Achievements

Frequently Asked Questions

How often can I claim?

You can claim once every 30 minutes.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

The minimum withdrawal amount is 0.00050000 USDT.

How does the referral system work?

You earn 20% of eligible faucet rewards earned by users who register through your referral link.

How does the achievement bonus work?

Users who accumulate 5 USDT in eligible faucet claim progress receive a 5 USDT achievement bonus. After the bonus is awarded, the achievement progress resets so the user can work toward the next bonus.

Which currency does Nexus Faucet pay?

Nexus Faucet uses USDT for faucet rewards and withdrawals.
